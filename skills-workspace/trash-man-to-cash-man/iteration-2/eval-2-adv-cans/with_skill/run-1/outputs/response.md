# Splitting $500 Using Myron Golden's Six Money Cans

From chapter 13 of *From The Trash Man To The Cash Man* (2007), "You Can If Your Cans Say You Can." Golden says to buy six actual paint cans and split **every** dollar that comes in, side income included.

## One question first: is $500 revenue or profit?
Golden says the tithe is 10% of **gross**. With side sales, "gross" could mean:
- **What buyers paid you** (revenue), or
- **What's left after you pay back the cost of the items you sold.**

If the $500 is all profit (for example, selling things you already owned), split all $500 as below. If some of it has to buy back inventory, set that money aside first as business money. Then split the rest. Whether to tithe on revenue or profit is your call, since the book is written around paychecks. **(applied)** The table below assumes the full $500.

## The split

| # | Can | % | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **I can tithe** | 10% | **$50** |
| 2 | **I can finish free** | 10% | **$50** |
| 3 | **I can educate myself** | 10% | **$50** |
| 4 | **I can save for what I want** | ~10%* | **$50** |
| 5 | **I can have fun** | ~10%* | **$50** |
| 6 | **I can pay my bills** | 50% | **$250** |
| | **Total** | 100% | **$500** |

\*The copy of the book I worked from clearly gives the percentages for cans 1, 2, 3 and 6 (10 + 10 + 10 + 50 = 80%). Cans 4 and 5 split the remaining **20%**. An even 10% / 10% is the most likely reading, but the exact numbers for those two weren't confirmed. You could also do 15/5 or 5/15 and still match the book.

## What each can is for

### 1. "I can tithe": $50
10% of gross goes to God's work through your church. Golden's view is that giving comes first, before anything else. He is writing from his Christian faith. If that isn't your belief, the book allows giving to charity instead.

### 2. "I can finish free": $50
This is your **financial freedom** money, and it has the strictest rule: **never spend it.** It's only used to buy **income producing assets (IPAs)** or businesses. You *can* spend the **profits** those assets make, but never the money in the can. It's how you move from the "poor" financial house (all income goes out the Outgo window) to the "rich" one (income buys assets that pay you). With side sales, this can might buy inventory or equipment that grows your sales, or go into investments. You let it build until it can buy something that pays you. Do your own research before any investment.

### 3. "I can educate myself": $50
For books, seminars and audio programs. Golden calls your mind your greatest asset. Two of his money traps are poor people "don't invest in their greatest asset" and "entertain themselves more than educate themselves." $50 a month buys a couple of books or a low-cost course on selling, marketing or money. (Golden also says "you're in the marketing business," and that's a good place to start.)

### 4. "I can save for what I want": $50
For bigger planned purchases and safety: vacations, down payments, furniture, emergencies. Unlike can #2, **you are meant to spend this one** on things you've saved toward. That way big costs don't go on credit cards. (Golden's trap #13: "don't use good debt; bad debt uses them.")

### 5. "I can have fun": $50
This one **must be spent every month** on something you enjoy. That's not optional. Golden includes it so you enjoy the trip to wealth instead of feeling deprived and quitting the system. If $50 is left at month's end, go spend it.

### 6. "I can pay my bills": $250
50% covers living costs: rent, utilities, food, transport and so on. Golden caps your lifestyle at half your income. Every other can is a way to build wealth, get better, or enjoy life.

## Practical tips (applied)
- **Use real containers or separate accounts.** Paint cans or envelopes for cash, or six sub-accounts or savings "buckets" at your bank for digital money. Keep can #2 especially hard to reach.
- **If this $500 is extra on top of a job that already covers your bills,** you still follow Golden's split. But consider the $250 bills portion as a way to pay down high-interest debt faster, a wealth reducing liability (WRL).
- **Do it every time money comes in,** not just this month. The habit matters more than the amount.
- **Side sales as a stepping stone:** Golden's Window of Opportunity ranks self-employment (O.A.J., Own A Job) as one of the weaker ways to make money, because the income stops when you stop. Can #2 is how side-sales money turns into S.M.S. (Sow Money Seeds) and eventually O.A.S. (Own A System).

## Caveats
- The 4th and 5th can percentages are inferred (see above).
- The tithe reflects the author's faith. Adapt it to your beliefs.
- Side-sale income may be taxable. You may need to set money aside for taxes before splitting.
- This is educational, not individual financial or tax advice.
