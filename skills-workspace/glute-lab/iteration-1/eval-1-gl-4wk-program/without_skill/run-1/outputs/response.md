# 4-Week Glute-Focused Program (Mon / Wed / Fri, about 60 min)

You're already strong for a glute program. A 185 x 8 hip thrust works out to an estimated max of about 230 lb, and a 135 x 5 squat to about 150–155 lb. So this plan uses real loads and real progression, not light band work.

**How it's set up**
- **Every day trains glutes**, but each day leads with a different main lift:
  - **Mon: heavy hip thrust + squat.** Heavy loads, with the glutes working hardest when the hips are fully extended at the top.
  - **Wed: hinge + single-leg.** This puts load on the glutes while they're stretched, which is a strong growth signal.
  - **Fri: hip thrust volume + abduction.** Higher reps, a strong pump, and work for the upper glutes (side and top of the hip).
- **About 12–16 hard glute sets per week**, which is the productive range for most intermediate lifters.
- **Two short upper-body pairings each day** so you stay balanced on a 3-day schedule. Skip them if you train upper body elsewhere.
- **Weeks 1–3 build, and Week 4 is a lighter week with a rep test.**

---

## Effort guide (RIR = reps in reserve)

RIR is how many more reps you could have done when you stop the set.

| Week | Main lifts | Accessories | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | RIR 3 (3 reps left) | RIR 2 | Groove technique and set your working weights |
| 2 | RIR 2 | RIR 1–2 | Add weight or reps |
| 3 | RIR 1 | RIR 0–1 (last set to failure on machines and bands) | Hardest week |
| 4 | RIR 3–4, sets cut by about 40% | RIR 2–3 | Lighter week. AMRAP (as many reps as possible) test on Friday |

**Rest:** 2–3 min on the main lifts, 60–90 s on accessories, 30–45 s between paired upper-body sets.

---

## Warm-up (5–7 min, every session)
- 1 x 20 glute bridges (bodyweight, pause at the top)
- 1 x 15 banded lateral walks each way, or 1 x 15 clamshells per side
- 1 x 10 bodyweight squats or goblet squats
- Ramp-up sets on the first lift: bar x 10, about 50% x 5, about 75% x 3, then your working sets

## MONDAY: Heavy Thrust + Squat

| # | Exercise | Wk 1 | Wk 2 | Wk 3 | Wk 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Barbell hip thrust** | 4 x 6 @ 175 | 4 x 6 @ 185 | 4 x 5 @ 195–200 | 2 x 6 @ 165 |
| B | **Back squat** | 3 x 6 @ 115 | 3 x 6 @ 120 | 4 x 5 @ 125–130 | 2 x 5 @ 105 |
| C | **Dumbbell reverse lunge** (deficit optional) | 3 x 10/leg | 3 x 10/leg (+5 lb per hand) | 3 x 12/leg | 2 x 10/leg |
| D1 | Lat pulldown | 3 x 10 | 3 x 10 | 3 x 12 | 2 x 10 |
| D2 | Dumbbell incline press | 3 x 10 | 3 x 10 | 3 x 12 | 2 x 10 |
| E | **Seated hip abduction machine** | 2 x 20 | 3 x 20 | 3 x 20 + 1 dropset | 2 x 15 |

**Hip thrust cues:** keep your chin tucked and look forward, not at the ceiling. Tilt the pelvis back slightly at the top so the ribs stay down and the lower back doesn't arch. Shins should be vertical at lockout. Pause 1 s at the top. If you feel it in your quads, move your feet a little farther out. If you feel it in your hamstrings, bring them closer.

**Squat:** sit down between your hips rather than back. A slightly wider stance with toes turned out about 15–30° gets the glutes more involved. Go as deep as you can while keeping your lower back neutral.

## WEDNESDAY: Hinge + Single-Leg

| # | Exercise | Wk 1 | Wk 2 | Wk 3 | Wk 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Romanian deadlift (barbell)** | 3 x 8 @ about 115 | 3 x 8 @ about 125 | 4 x 8 @ about 135 | 2 x 8 @ about 105 |
| B | **Bulgarian split squat** (dumbbells, slight forward lean) | 3 x 8/leg | 3 x 10/leg | 3 x 10/leg (heavier) | 2 x 8/leg |
| C | **45° back extension, glute-biased** (round the upper back, toes out, push hips into the pad) | 3 x 15 | 3 x 15 (hold a plate) | 3 x 12 (heavier) + last set to failure | 2 x 12 |
| D1 | One-arm dumbbell row | 3 x 10/side | 3 x 10/side | 3 x 12/side | 2 x 10/side |
| D2 | Seated dumbbell shoulder press | 3 x 10 | 3 x 10 | 3 x 12 | 2 x 10 |
| E | **Cable kickback** (lean slightly forward, kick back and slightly out) | 2 x 15/leg | 3 x 15/leg | 3 x 15/leg | 2 x 12/leg |

**RDL starting weight:** begin around 95–115 lb and use the heaviest weight you can move with a flat back and a full hamstring stretch, bar just below the knee or mid-shin. Use a 3-second lowering. Stretch under load is the point of this lift, so don't rush the descent.

**Bulgarians:** a longer stance and a forward torso lean shift more of the work to the glutes. Start with your weaker leg and match the reps on the other side.

## FRIDAY: Thrust Volume + Upper Glutes

| # | Exercise | Wk 1 | Wk 2 | Wk 3 | Wk 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Barbell hip thrust (or glute bridge)** | 3 x 12 @ 145 | 3 x 12 @ 155 | 3 x 12 @ 160–165 | **AMRAP test: 1 x max reps @ 185** |
| B | **Leg press, feet high and wide** | 3 x 12 | 3 x 12 | 3 x 15 | 2 x 12 |
| C | **B-stance (kickstand) dumbbell RDL** | 3 x 10/leg | 3 x 10/leg | 3 x 12/leg | 2 x 10/leg |
| D1 | Seated cable row | 3 x 12 | 3 x 12 | 3 x 12 | 2 x 12 |
| D2 | Push-ups (incline if needed) | 3 x AMRAP-2 (stop 2 short of max) | 3 x AMRAP-2 | 3 x AMRAP-1 | 2 x 8 |
| E | **Glute finisher:** 1 round = 20 banded frog pumps + 20 banded seated abductions + 20 banded lateral walks | 2 rounds | 2 rounds | 3 rounds | 1 round |

**Week 4 test:** after a good warm-up, hip thrust 185 for as many clean reps as you can, with a full lockout and a 1-second pause on every rep. Then do 2 x 12 at 135 and the rest of a light session. If you beat 8 reps, the program worked. Use your new numbers to set the next block.

---

## Progression rules
1. **Double progression on accessories:** pick a weight and work within the rep range. Once you hit the top of the range on every set at the target RIR, add weight next week (5 lb on dumbbells, 5–10 lb on machines and cables).
2. **The barbell numbers above are targets, not rules.** If a week feels clearly harder than the target RIR (bad sleep, stress, your cycle), repeat the previous week's weight. If it feels easier, go up 5–10 lb.
3. **Form is the limit.** Hip thrusts need a full lockout and squats need consistent depth. A rep that loses either doesn't count.
4. **Log everything:** weight, reps and how hard the last set felt. Progress is what you can measure.

---

## Getting the most out of it
- **Protein:** about 0.7–1 g per lb of bodyweight per day, spread over 3–4 meals.
- **Calories:** to grow your glutes noticeably, eat at maintenance or a small surplus (+150–250 kcal per day). In a deficit you can still get stronger and keep muscle, but growth will be slower.
- **Steps and recovery:** 7,000–10,000 steps a day and 7+ hours of sleep. Don't add hard leg conditioning on Tuesday or Thursday. Light cardio or walking is fine.
- **Mind-muscle connection:** on the accessories, slow down, squeeze at the point where the muscle is shortest, and actually feel your glutes working. On the main lifts, focus on moving the weight well.
- **Equipment substitutions:**
  - No hip thrust bench or station: use a flat bench and a barbell pad, or a Smith machine.
  - No abduction machine: banded seated abductions or cable standing abductions.
  - No back extension station: cable pull-throughs.
- **Pain is different from effort.** Sharp pain in the knee, back or hip means stop the lift and swap it for a pain-free variation. A deep muscle burn is fine.

## After Week 4
Run the block again with your hip thrust loads raised by 10–15 lb and your squat by 5–10 lb. You can also rotate the main lifts:
- Front squat or high-bar pause squat in place of the back squat
- Trap-bar deadlift in place of the RDL
- Single-leg hip thrust in place of the Friday thrust

Changing the lifts every 4–8 weeks keeps you progressing and keeps the plan interesting.
