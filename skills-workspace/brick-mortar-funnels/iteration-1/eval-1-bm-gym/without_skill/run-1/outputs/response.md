# 100 Gym Members in 100 Days on a $1,000 Budget (the Hormozi Approach)

**A note before the plan:** I can't check the exact wording or figures in a book called *Brick & Mortar Funnels*. What follows is the gym playbook Alex Hormozi has taught publicly through Gym Launch, *Gym Launch Secrets*, *$100M Offers* and *$100M Leads*. Every number below is a typical benchmark or a worked example, not a quote. Replace them with your own figures after the first two weeks.

---

## 1. The core idea: let new members pay for the ads

Hormozi doesn't treat $1,000 as the whole budget. He treats it as **seed money**. The goal is **client-financed acquisition**: the money a new member pays upfront should cover what it cost to get them, ideally within 30 days. Then you put that cash straight back into more ads.

- **Target:** front-end cash collected per member is at least 2x your cost to acquire a customer (CAC).
- **Long-term health check:** lifetime value (LTV) to CAC of 3:1 or better.
- **What it means for you:** the $1,000 only has to get the first round going. After that, each round pays for the next.

## 2. The offer: a paid, time-boxed challenge rather than a discounted membership

Don't advertise "$49/month, first month free." Sell a **Grand Slam Offer**, the classic "6-Week Challenge":

| Element | Example |
|---|---|
| Dream outcome | "Lose 10-20 lbs in 6 weeks" (or a strength or energy goal for your market) |
| Price (front-end) | $299-$499 paid in full upfront |
| Stack | Unlimited classes, nutrition plan, weekly check-ins, accountability group, before/after assessment |
| Guarantee | Hit the attendance and nutrition-logging requirements but miss the goal, and you get your money back (a conditional guarantee) |
| Scarcity | "Only 20 spots this round," which is true because you cap it |
| Urgency | Challenge start date is fixed, and enrollment closes on that date |
| Back end | In week 4-5, offer the continuation membership ($150-$200/mo). That is where long-term profit comes from. |

The value equation is **(Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay x Effort)**. A guaranteed result in a short, fixed window makes the outcome feel likely and the wait short, so the price feels small.

## 3. The funnel

**Ad (Facebook/Instagram, 5-10 mile radius) → short lead form or landing page → call or text within 5 minutes → booked consult → show-up → in-person close → challenge → upsell to membership → referrals**

Hormozi's rules for the parts gyms usually get wrong:

- **Speed to lead:** contact every lead within 5 minutes. Make up to 6-8 call and text attempts over the first 2-3 days.
- **Confirm appointments.** Send a text plus a reminder 24 hours and 2 hours before. A good show rate is worth more than cheaper leads.
- **Close in person.** Do a goals-and-obstacles consult, then present the challenge. Collect payment on the spot.
- **Ask for referrals at the peak.** Ask right after sign-up and again when they see first results. Use a "bring a friend free for week 1" or "buddy challenge" offer.

## 4. The funnel numbers on $1,000

Typical local fitness benchmarks. Use the middle column as the plan.

| Stage | Conservative | Plan | Good |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost per lead (CPL) | $25 | **$15** | $8 |
| Leads from $1,000 | 40 | **~66** | 125 |
| Lead → booked appointment | 40% | **60%** | 70% |
| Booked → showed | 50% | **65%** | 80% |
| Showed → closed | 40% | **50%** | 60% |
| **New members** | **~3** | **~13** | **~42** |
| CAC (cost per member) | ~$313 | **~$77** | ~$24 |
| Front-end cash at $299 | ~$960 | **~$3,890** | ~$12,560 |

Plan-column math: $1,000 / $15 = 66 leads → x 0.60 = 40 booked → x 0.65 = 26 shows → x 0.50 = **13 members**. 13 x $299 = **$3,887 collected**, which is about **3.9x the ad spend** in the first round.

The conservative column only breaks even, so sales follow-up matters more than the ad. If your close rate or show rate is low, fix that before you spend more.

## 5. The 100-day plan (target: about 1 member per day, about 7 per week)

Run challenge rounds in 2- to 3-week enrollment waves and reinvest about half of the front-end cash each wave. Keep the other half to deliver the service and pay yourself.

| Phase | Days | Ad spend | Paid members | Referrals & organic | Running total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Setup** | 1-7 | $0 | 0 | Warm outreach (see below) → **5** | 5 |
| **1. Seed round** | 8-28 | $1,000 | **13** | 0.3 referrals/member → **4** | 22 |
| **2. Reinvest** | 29-56 | $2,000 (from round 1 cash) | **26** | **8** referrals + **4** outreach | 60 |
| **3. Scale** | 57-90 | $2,500 (from round 2 cash) | **32** | **10** referrals | 102 |
| **4. Convert** | 91-100 | $0 new | — | Move challengers onto memberships | **~100+** |

Totals: **$5,500 total ad spend, only $1,000 of it from your own pocket.** You get about 71 paid-ad members plus about 31 from referrals and outreach, so **~100 members**. About **$30,000 in front-end revenue** at $299 each.

Then the back end: if **60-70% of challengers renew** at about $150/mo, you finish with **~60-70 recurring members, or about $9,000-$10,500 in new monthly recurring revenue**, along with the other challengers still finishing.

## 6. The free channels: Hormozi's "Core Four"

Paid ads are only one of the four channels in *$100M Leads*. On a tiny budget you work the free ones every day:

1. **Warm outreach (free):** go through everyone in your phone and social contacts. Message 100 people a day. Don't pitch them. Say something like *"I'm running a 6-week challenge and looking for a few people who want to get in shape. Know anyone?"* Even 1-2% converting gives 5-10 members in week one.
2. **Content (free):** post member transformations, short coaching tips and challenge updates daily.
3. **Cold outreach (free, local):** partner with nearby businesses such as salons, chiropractors, offices and coffee shops. Offer free "employee challenge" spots or give them a referral bounty.
4. **Paid ads:** the $1,000 seed described above.

He also calls this the **"Rule of 100":** do 100 primary actions a day (100 outreach messages, or 100 minutes of content, or $100/day in ads) for 100 days.

## 7. Key metrics to track weekly

- CPL (target under $15-20)
- Lead-to-booked % (target 60%+)
- Show rate (target 65%+)
- Close rate (target 50%+)
- CAC versus front-end cash per member (target 2x or better in 30 days)
- Challenge-to-membership conversion (target 60%+)
- Referrals per member (target 0.3+)

## Bottom line

You won't buy 100 members with $1,000. You'll buy about **13 members**, and their **~$3,900** buys the next 26. Do that 3 times, add referrals and 100 daily outreach messages, and you reach **~100 members in ~100 days**. Everything rests on a high-value, guaranteed, upfront-paid challenge offer and fast, persistent follow-up.
