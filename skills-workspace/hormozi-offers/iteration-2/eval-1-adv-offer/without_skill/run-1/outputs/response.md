# Grand Slam Offer: Garage Cleanout & Junk Hauling

The goal is to stop selling "a load for $300" and make an offer so good that people feel stupid saying no. If you sell what competitors sell, you get compared on price. If you build something nobody else offers, there is nothing to compare it to.

---

## 1. Dream Outcome

Nobody wants junk hauled. They want what they get afterward:

> **"Park your car in your garage again, and open the door without being embarrassed, by this weekend, without lifting a finger."**

Layers of the dream outcome to use in your copy:
- **Functional:** a clean, usable garage (car fits, tools findable, room for a gym or workshop).
- **Emotional:** relief, pride, an end to arguments with a spouse about "the garage."
- **Status:** neighbors see the door open and it looks like a showroom.
- **Life events:** getting ready to sell the house, a move, a new baby, a parent moving in, an estate cleanout.

### Value equation check
Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay x Effort and Sacrifice)

| Lever | Where competitors are | Where you will be |
|---|---|---|
| Dream outcome | "We remove stuff" | "Your garage back" |
| Likelihood | Unknown crew, vague pricing | Guaranteed results, photos, reviews, insured |
| Time delay | "We'll call you back," a week out | Quote in 15 minutes, done in one day |
| Effort and sacrifice | Customer sorts, carries, schedules, donates | Customer points; you do everything |

## 2. Problems (every obstacle the customer faces)

List every problem before, during and after the job. Each one becomes a solution.

**Before**
1. "I don't know what it'll cost." (fear of a surprise bill)
2. "I don't have time to be home all day."
3. "I can't decide what to keep. It's overwhelming."
4. "I don't trust strangers in my house / garage."
5. "Getting quotes is a pain; nobody calls back."
6. "There's hazardous stuff (paint, chemicals, batteries) and I don't know what to do with it."

**During**
7. "They'll damage my floor, drywall or driveway."
8. "They'll toss something I wanted to keep."
9. "It'll take forever."
10. "They'll leave a mess."

**After**
11. "It'll be cluttered again in six months."
12. "I wanted my stuff donated, not dumped, and to get a tax receipt."
13. "Now I have an empty garage but no system: things still pile on the floor."
14. "The floor is stained and gross."
15. "I could have sold some of this stuff."

## 4. Delivery Vehicles (how each solution is delivered)

For each solution, ask: done-for-you, done-with-you, or do-it-yourself? One-on-one or group? In person or digital? How fast?

- **Done-for-you (core):** crew does the full cleanout, sort, haul, donate, and clean.
- **Done-with-you (option):** 30-minute walk-through with the owner to tag Keep / Donate / Toss.
- **Digital:** photo quote by text; before/after photo report; donation receipts by email.
- **Physical:** shelving/hook install; labeled storage bins.
- **Ongoing:** 6-month reset visit; annual cleanout membership.
- **Speed tiers:** standard (within 7 days), priority (next day), emergency (same day / moving deadline).

1. Full garage cleanout, every item hauled, in one visit
2. Crew sorts everything into Keep / Donate / Toss (you just approve)
3. "Hold bin" for anything you're unsure about
4. Donation drop-off with tax receipt emailed
5. Small hazardous items (paint, batteries, chemicals) disposed properly
6. Full sweep, blow-out, and cobweb removal
7. Before/after photo report
8. You don't need to be home
9. **Bonus:** 6-month "Reset Visit" (one free light load + tidy)
10. **Bonus:** "Keep it clean" checklist and zone map taped inside the garage

## 6. Pricing

Do not compete with the $300 load. You're not selling a load; you're selling a garage back. Charge a premium and use that margin to deliver more, so the customer is happier and you can pay a better crew.

**Value anchor (what it would cost to piece together):**
- Junk removal for a full garage (2-3 loads): $600-$900
- Professional organizer, half day: $300-$500
- Donation pickup coordination + hazardous disposal: $100-$150
- Floor clean: $150
- Follow-up visit: $150
- **Total value: roughly $1,300-$1,850**

**Tiers (good / better / best):**

| Tier | Includes | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Clear-Out** | Haul-away + sort + hold bin + sweep + donation receipt | $795 |
| **Garage Rescue** (most popular) | Everything above + hazardous disposal + photo report + 6-month reset visit + degrease/pressure wash | $1,295 |
| **Garage Makeover** | Everything above + wall shelving/hooks install + labeled bins + 12-month reset | $2,495 |

Notes:
- The middle tier is the one you want people to pick; the top tier makes it look reasonable.
- Price by garage size (1-car / 2-car / 3-car) instead of by load, so the customer never worries about the truck "filling up."
- Add a **rush fee** (+$200 for next-day, +$350 same-day) and a **moving deadline guarantee** for realtors and sellers.
- Upsells: epoxy floor (partner), recurring annual cleanout membership, attic or basement add-on, resale service.
- Offer a **realtor program**: listing agents get a pre-sale garage package they can gift or bill at close.

## 7. Guarantee

Stack guarantees to remove risk. Pick one strong headline guarantee and back it with smaller ones.

- **Headline (conditional, unconditional feel):** *"Garage done in one day, or it's free."* If we don't finish in the single scheduled visit, you don't pay.
- **Price-lock guarantee:** "The price we quote from your photos is the price you pay. No surprise fees, ever."
- **Damage guarantee:** "If we scratch, dent or break anything, we fix or replace it."
- **Spotless guarantee:** "If the garage isn't broom-clean and you're not happy, we come back free within 48 hours."
- **Nothing-lost guarantee:** "Anything you're unsure about goes in the hold bin. We never toss it without your say-so."

Making it a strong guarantee is fine because the risk is controllable: you only quote what you can finish, you set the scope from photos, and you use a "one-day" promise matched to crew size.

## 8. MAGIC Name

MAGIC = **M**agnetic reason why, **A**vatar (who it's for), **G**oal (dream outcome), **I**nterval (time frame), **C**ontainer word (package name).

Options:
- **"The 1-Day Garage Rescue"** (Interval + Container + Goal)
- **"Spring Reset: Get Your Car Back in the Garage in 1 Day"** (Reason + Goal + Interval)
- **"Homeowner's Park-It-Again Garage Makeover: Done in a Day or It's Free"** (Avatar + Goal + Interval + Container + guarantee)
- **"Pre-Sale Garage Blitz for Home Sellers: Show-Ready in 24 Hours"** (Avatar + Goal + Interval + Container, for the realtor/seller niche)
- **"New-Year Garage Reset: 1-Day Cleanout, Organize & Haul"** (seasonal reason why)

**Recommended headline offer:**
> **"The 1-Day Garage Rescue: Park in Your Garage Again by Tomorrow Night, or It's Free."**

Rotate the magnetic reason seasonally (Spring Reset, Before-the-Holidays, Moving Season, Tax-Season Donation) so the offer stays fresh without rebuilding it.

## 9. Putting it all together (the pitch)

> "Most guys charge $300 a load and leave you to sort, carry and clean. We do the whole garage in one day. Text us three photos, get a fixed price in 15 minutes. Our crew sorts everything into keep, donate and toss. Anything you're not sure about goes in a hold bin. We drop donations at the local charity and email you the tax receipt, take the old paint and chemicals, and blow out and pressure wash the floor. You don't even have to be home. You get before and after photos, and in six months we come back for a free reset. If we don't finish in one day, it's free. If we damage anything, we fix it. The Garage Rescue is $1,295 for a two-car garage. Do you want this Saturday or next Tuesday?"

### Next steps
1. Test the middle tier at $1,295 with your next 10 leads; track close rate.
2. If more than ~40% say yes immediately, raise the price.
3. Collect before/after photos and video testimonials on every job; they boost perceived likelihood more than anything.
4. Build the realtor channel: one agent can send you a steady stream of pre-sale jobs.
