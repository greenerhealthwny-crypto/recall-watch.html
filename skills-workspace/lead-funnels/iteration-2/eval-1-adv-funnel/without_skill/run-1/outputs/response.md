# Lead Funnel: Local Furniture Flipper (Facebook Marketplace)

## 0. Strategy in one paragraph

A Marketplace flipper's problem is that every listing is a one-off: a buyer messages, buys a dresser (or doesn't), and is gone. The funnel turns those one-off conversations, plus cheap local ad traffic, into an **owned list** (SMS + email) of local people who furnish homes. Then every new piece goes to that list first, before it hits Marketplace. That gives faster sales, fewer lowballers, repeat buyers and referrals. Secondary audience: **sellers** (people moving, estates, landlords) who will offer you inventory cheaply. The funnel below is for buyers. A short seller variant comes at the end.

**Avatar:** a local 25–45-year-old renter or first-time homeowner, or a young family. They want solid-wood or mid-century or "looks expensive" furniture without paying retail. They're nervous about quality, smell, bugs and pickup logistics.

---

## 1. Lead Magnet

**Name:** *"The VIP Drop List: First Look at Restored Pieces Before They Hit Marketplace"*, bundled with a free PDF: *"The 7-Point Used Furniture Checklist: How to Spot Solid Wood, Bedbugs, and Overpriced Junk in 60 Seconds."*

Why this works:
- **Specific and local.** Only people near you want it, so the list is pre-qualified.
- **Fixes a real fear.** Buying used is scary, and the checklist builds trust that you know quality.
- **Ongoing value.** Early access, 24–48 hours before the public listing, is the real hook. The PDF is the immediate gratification.
- **Cheap to make.** One 2–3 page PDF plus a weekly text.

Alternates to test:
- "Free room-styling consult: send a photo of your space, get 3 piece suggestions under $300."
- A monthly $50 store-credit giveaway for list members (only use this as a list-builder, because it attracts freebie-seekers).

Entry points (traffic sources):
1. Every Marketplace listing description: "Want first dibs on pieces like this? Text DROP to (555) 123-4567 or join at [link]."
2. Every buyer conversation, at pickup: "I post new pieces to my VIP list first. Want on it?" (collect the phone number on the spot).
3. A QR code sticker on the underside or back of every sold piece, plus a business card in drawers.
4. Local FB/IG ads ($5–10/day, 10–15 mile radius) showing before/after flips.
5. Local FB groups, Nextdoor and buy-nothing groups (value posts, not spam).

## 2. Landing (Opt-in) Page

Mobile-first, one page, one action.

**Headline:**
> Get First Dibs on Restored Solid-Wood Furniture in [City], Before It Hits Marketplace

**Subhead:**
> Join 400+ locals on the VIP Drop List. New pieces every week, 24 hours early, at VIP prices. Plus a free 7-Point Used Furniture Checklist.

**Hero visual:** a before/after slider of one dramatic flip (a $40 curb dresser that became a $325 showpiece).

**Bullets:**
- See every new piece **24 hours before the public**, so the best ones never reach Marketplace
- **VIP-only pricing** (usually 10% below the listed price)
- Free checklist: spot solid wood vs. particleboard, bedbug signs, and fair prices
- Local delivery available, so no begging a friend with a truck

**Form:** First name, mobile number (required, with an SMS consent checkbox), email (optional). Button: **"Send Me the VIP List →"**

**Trust:**
- 3 short reviews with first name and neighborhood ("Bought a mid-century credenza, Sarah, [Neighborhood]")
- "Every piece is cleaned, inspected and smoke-free"
- A photo of you or your workspace (local, human, real)
- "Unsubscribe anytime. Max 2 texts a week."

Tools: Carrd, Leadpages, Systeme.io or a ConvertKit landing page. SMS via SimpleTexting, Textedly or EZ Texting. The form connects to both email and SMS.

## 3. Thank-You Page

Its job is to confirm, deliver, set expectations and make an immediate offer.

**Headline:** "You're in! Check your texts."

1. **Delivery:** a "Download the 7-Point Checklist" button, which is also sent by text and email.
2. **Expectations:** "Drops go out Thursdays at 7 pm. Save my number as *[Name] Furniture* so you don't miss one."
3. **Micro-commitment:** "What are you hunting for?" with one-tap buttons: Dresser / Dining set / Sofa / Desk / Accent chair / Bed frame / Other. This tags the lead for segmented alerts.
4. **Immediate offer (tripwire / self-liquidator):** "Available right now for VIPs only:" a gallery of 3–6 current pieces with VIP prices and a **"Claim It — $20 Hold Deposit"** button (Stripe, Square or Venmo link). The deposit is credited to the purchase and refundable if the piece isn't as described.
5. **Referral:** "Know someone moving or furnishing? Share this link, and you both get $15 off your next piece."

## 4. Sales Page / Offer

There are two layers: the **per-piece sales page** for each drop, and a **core paid offer** that increases average order value.

### 4a. Weekly Drop page (the recurring sales page)
A simple gallery page (or a Shopify / Square Online "Drop" collection) updated every Thursday:
- Each piece: 3–5 photos (styled, natural light), dimensions, material, condition notes, before photo, VIP price vs. public price, and a "Hold it: $20 deposit" button
- Scarcity that's real: "One of one. When it's gone, it's gone." Show "SOLD" badges on pieces that have sold (social proof that drops move fast)
- Delivery upsell: "+$35 local delivery & placement (within 10 miles)"
- Bundle upsell: "Add the matching nightstand: save $40"

### 4b. Core / premium offers (move beyond one-piece-at-a-time)
| Offer | Price | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Room Refresh Bundle**: 3–5 coordinated pieces for a bedroom or living room, delivered and placed | $600–$1,500 | New movers, Airbnb hosts |
| **Custom Flip / Sourcing Request**: "Tell me what you want, I'll find and restore it in 2–3 weeks" ($50 deposit) | $50 deposit, then piece price | People with specific needs |
| **Refinish Your Own Piece**: customer brings an heirloom and you paint or refinish it | $150–$400 | Sentimental owners |
| **Staging Rental** for realtors and landlords | $300–$800/month | B2B (higher value, recurring) |

**Sales-page copy skeleton for the Room Refresh Bundle:**
- Headline: "Furnish Your Whole Bedroom With Restored Solid Wood for Less Than One IKEA Dresser Costs New"
- Problem: particleboard that wobbles in a year, retail prices, weekend-long pickups
- Solution and proof: before/after gallery, testimonials, price comparison table (retail vs. you)
- Offer stack: pieces, cleaning and inspection, delivery, placement, 7-day "not as described" guarantee
- Guarantee: "If anything isn't as described when it arrives, I'll take it back, no hassle."
- CTA: "Book a free 10-minute style call" or "Reserve with a $100 deposit"

## 5. Follow-Up Sequence

The channels are SMS (primary, because local buyers act fast) and email (secondary, for richer content).

### Welcome / indoctrination (first 7 days)
| Day | Channel | Message |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (instant) | SMS + email | "Hey {first}, it's [Name]. Here's your checklist: [link]. Drops every Thu 7 pm. Reply with what you're hunting for and I'll flag it for you." |
| 1 | Email | Your story: "How a $15 curbside dresser started this," with before/after photos and why you only flip solid wood |
| 2 | SMS | "Quick one: this walnut nightstand is VIP-only until Fri at $85 (going to Marketplace at $110). Want it? [link]" |
| 3 | Email | Value: "3 ways to tell solid wood in 10 seconds" plus a testimonial |
| 5 | Email | Social proof: "What Sarah's living room looks like now," a customer photo and the Room Refresh Bundle |
| 7 | SMS | "Drop tonight at 7! 6 new pieces incl. a mid-century credenza. First look: [link]" |

### Ongoing broadcast (weekly rhythm)
- **Wednesday:** teaser SMS: "Tomorrow's drop has a [farmhouse table]. Set a reminder 👀" (keep emojis minimal and on-brand)
- **Thursday 7 pm:** drop SMS and email, the main sales event
- **Saturday:** "Last chance before these go public on Marketplace Sunday" SMS
- **Monthly:** behind-the-scenes email (a time-lapse of a restoration) plus a referral reminder

### Segmented / behavioral triggers
- **Tagged "dresser" and a dresser comes in:** personal SMS 2 hours before the drop: "You said you wanted a dresser. This one's yours if you want it."
- **Clicked but didn't buy:** next day, "Still thinking about the oak desk? Happy to send more photos or measurements."
- **Bought:** day 1 thank-you plus care tips; day 7, "How's it looking? Send a pic for a $10 credit" (UGC and reviews); day 30, referral ask plus the bundle offer
- **No clicks in 60 days:** re-engagement: "Still want drops? Reply YES to stay on." Remove anyone who doesn't reply (protects SMS costs and deliverability)

### Seller-side mini funnel (inventory sourcing)
Lead magnet: "Moving? Get a cash offer on your furniture in 24 hours. Text photos to [number]." Follow-up: instant auto-reply, then a quote within 24 hours, then pickup scheduling. List them separately. Realtors, property managers and movers are referral partners.

## 6. List Math

Assumptions (conservative, local; adjust these with your real numbers):

| Metric | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Landing page visitors/month (ads + listings + QR + groups) | 1,000 |
| Opt-in rate (local, specific offer, mobile) | 30% |
| **New subscribers/month** | **300** |
| Ad spend | $250/mo ($8/day) |
| Cost per lead (blended, organic leads are free) | ~$0.83 |
| Monthly list churn/unsubscribes | 5% |
| % of list buying in a given month | 4% |
| Average order value (piece + delivery/upsell) | $180 |
| Average gross margin per order (sale − acquisition cost − materials) | ~55% → ~$100 |

**Month-by-month list growth** (add 300, lose 5%):

| Month | List size (end) | Buyers (4%) | Revenue (@$180) | Gross profit (@$100) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 300 | 12 | $2,160 | $1,200 |
| 3 | ~856 | 34 | $6,120 | $3,400 |
| 6 | ~1,600 | 64 | $11,520 | $6,400 |
| 12 | ~2,770 | 111 | $19,980 | $11,100 |

(Formula: List(n) = List(n−1) × 0.95 + 300; it levels off around 300 / 0.05 = 6,000.)

**Key unit economics:**
- **Value per subscriber per month** = 4% × $100 profit = **$4.00 gross profit**
- **Cost per subscriber** ≈ $0.83, so a subscriber pays back in about a week of list life
- **Subscriber lifetime** at 5% churn is about 20 months, so **lifetime value is about $80 gross profit per subscriber**
- **Maximum affordable CPL**: even at a $5 CPL you'd see 16:1 LTV:CAC on gross profit, so you can scale ads well before the math breaks

**The real constraint is inventory, not leads.** At 111 buyers/month by month 12 you need about 25–30 pieces per week, more than one person can source and restore. Plan for it:
- Once the list passes about 1,000, **raise prices 10–20%**. Demand from the list outruns supply, and pieces sell in hours.
- Shift toward **bundles, custom sourcing and staging rentals**, which give more revenue per piece of work.
- Put ad spend into the **seller-side funnel** to secure cheaper, steadier inventory.

**Sanity check on the tripwire / self-liquidating step:** if 5% of new subscribers place a $20 hold deposit that converts to an average $150 sale, then 300 subs × 5% = 15 sales × ~$80 profit ≈ $1,200/month on the front end alone. That's roughly 4–5x the $250 ad spend, so the ads pay for themselves before the list's back-end value is counted.

## 7. KPIs to track weekly
1. Opt-in rate (landing page), target 25%+
2. Cost per lead, target under $2
3. Drop-night click rate (SMS), target 15–25%
4. Sell-through within 48 hours of a drop, target 60%+ (if higher, raise prices)
5. Revenue per subscriber per month
6. Repeat purchase rate and referrals per month
7. Inventory days-on-hand

## 8. Tech stack (cheap)
- Landing and thank-you pages: Carrd ($19/yr) or Systeme.io (free tier)
- Email: MailerLite or Kit (free up to 1,000 subs)
- SMS: SimpleTexting or Textedly (~$30–60/mo). Get explicit consent and include STOP language (TCPA compliance)
- Payments and deposits: Square or Stripe payment links
- Drop gallery: Square Online, Shopify Starter or a simple Google Photos album link
- Tracking: UTM links per source (listing, QR, ad, group) so you know which channel builds the list
