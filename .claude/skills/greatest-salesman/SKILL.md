# The Greatest Salesman in the World (Og Mandino, 1968)

A working guide to Og Mandino's *The Greatest Salesman in the World* (Frederick Fell, 1968; the user's copy is the Bantam edition, with a preview of *Part II: The End of the Story* at the end) *(book)*.

**Source:** the user's PDF has a text layer (75 pages). All points below were checked against it *(book)* unless marked *(std)*.

## The story (frame for the scrolls)

- **Hafid**, now the old, wealthy "greatest salesman in the world" in Damascus, gives away his fortune and tells his loyal bookkeeper **Erasmus** the secret of his success *(book)*.
- Flashback: as a **camel boy** for the merchant **Pathros of Palmyra**, Hafid asks to become a salesman. Pathros tests him by sending him to **Bethlehem** to sell one fine red robe *(book)*.
- After failing for days, Hafid gives the robe to keep a **newborn infant in a cave warm** and rides back under the brightest star he has ever seen, believing he has failed *(book)*.
- Pathros, seeing the star follow him, recognizes the sign he was waiting for and gives Hafid a chest of **ten scrolls** that hold the principles of success *(book)*.
- Hafid applies them and becomes rich. In old age he waits for the person the scrolls are meant for next. He is **Paul (Saul of Tarsus)**, who arrives carrying the robe from Jesus' crucifixion. It bears the marks of Tola and Pathros: it is the very robe Hafid gave away *(book)*.

## The ten scrolls (one-line summary)

| Scroll | Principle | Signature line (short) |
|---|---|---|
| I | **Form good habits** and become their slave; the reading method itself is the first habit | "Today I begin a new life." |
| II | **Love**: the greatest secret of success; silently say "I love you" to everyone you meet; praise, never gossip | "I will greet this day with love in my heart." |
| III | **Persistence**: law of averages; each "no" brings you closer to "yes"; make one more attempt before going home | "I will persist until I succeed." |
| IV | **Self-worth**: you are unique; capitalize on your difference | "I am nature's greatest miracle." |
| V | **Urgency**: waste nothing on yesterday or tomorrow; make every hour count | "I will live this day as if it is my last." |
| VI | **Emotional control**: moods cycle like nature; counter each feeling with an action ("If I feel depressed I will sing…"); read the moods of others | "Today I will be master of my emotions." |
| VII | **Laughter and perspective**: laugh at failures, successes and yourself; "This too shall pass" | "I will laugh at the world." |
| VIII | **Goals and growth**: set goals for day, week, month, year and life at a hundredfold of your best; surpass your own deeds, not others' | "Today I will multiply my value a hundredfold." |
| IX | **Action**: dreams and plans are worthless without action; repeat the phrase hourly until it is a habit | "I will act now." |
| X | **Prayer for guidance**: ask not for gold but for ability equal to your opportunities | the salesman's prayer, "Guide me." |

## The method (scroll I) *(book)*

1. Read **one scroll for 30 days** before moving on.
2. Each day: read it **silently when you arise**, **silently after the midday meal**, and **aloud just before you retire**.
3. Do not skip a day: "that day cannot be retrieved."
4. Ten scrolls × 30 days ≈ **ten months**. Repetition sinks the words into the "other mind" (the subconscious) until the reactions become habit.

## How to use this skill

- **Summaries or teaching:** use the table and `references/scrolls.md`.
- **Coaching a salesperson or anyone facing rejection:** match the problem to the scroll (rejection → III; slump or mood → VI and VII; procrastination → IX and V; low confidence → IV; no direction → VIII; hard relationships → II).
- **Build a routine:** give the 30-day schedule, the scroll's key line and 2–3 concrete behaviors (for example, scroll III: "end each day with one more call").
- Keep quotations short. The book is in copyright; paraphrase longer passages *(std)*.

## Caveats

- It is an inspirational parable with religious themes (the infant in Bethlehem, Paul, prayer). Present the spiritual parts respectfully and as the book's, and adapt to the user's beliefs *(std)*.
- Its tips are motivational, not a sales technique manual. Pair with practical skills (prospecting, offers, follow-up) when needed *(std)*.
