## The five parts of a lead funnel

| Part | Purpose | Key rules |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Lead magnet** | Attract leads by giving value | Make it simple (Brendon Burchard's one-page "1-Page Productivity Planner"). Make many and test, since you don't know what will hit. The same magnet can carry different titles. Give it **curiosity, a "hook"**. Focus on **pain**: most people move away from pain, while entrepreneurs wrongly assume people move toward pleasure (Myron Golden's tip) |
| **2. Landing page** | Exchange the magnet for an email address | Three elements: an **intriguing headline, an image of the lead magnet, and an opt-in form**. Keep it simple and spend 5–10 minutes building it, then test and tweak. A good page converts about **20–30%**; the Marketing Secrets Blackbook page converted **55.63%** |
| **3. Thank-you page** | Deliver on the promise (trust), then make a first offer | Two objectives only: deliver the magnet (ideally a download link right on the page) and push to the next step. That can be a product, or for beginners a podcast, YouTube channel or blog. **No bait-and-switch** |
| **4. Sales page** | Sell the product, recoup ad costs, build a *customer* list | Use a **self-liquidating offer (SLO)** priced so that front-end sales pay for the ads. You can skip it until you have a product, or sell an affiliate offer |
| **5. Follow-up funnel** | Build the relationship | Automated email, SMS and Messenger messages plus broadcasts. Contacts are scored by **RFMS** (recency, frequency, monetary, social) |

**The core sequence:** build the list, then build the relationship. "Done is better than perfect": get to 80% and go *(book)*.

## Key principles and math

- **"The money is in the list."** The list is the only real asset. eBay bought Skype (about 60M users) for $2.6B and Facebook bought Instagram (30M users, no revenue) for $1B to get the users *(book)*.
- **List math:** done poorly, expect about **$1 per name per month**, so 1,000 names is about $1,000 a month and 100,000 names about $1.2M a year. With a strong relationship, each name can be worth $2–$50 a month. These are estimates, not guarantees *(book)*.
- **Ad math:** leads typically cost **$1–$3** in Brunson's market *(book)*.
  - **SLO example:** $100 of ads brings 100 leads. If 10% buy a $10 product, that's $100 back, so you have break-even "free" leads.
  - **Break-even day:** if only 5% buy at first, follow-up can bring another 5% by day 3, so you break even on day 3.
  - **By lead value:** at $1 per name per month and a $3 lead cost, you break even in month 3.
- **Pain-point lead magnet formula:** "The (#) Biggest Mistakes (audience) Make (when doing X)". Myron Golden's "12 Biggest Mistakes Network Marketers Make" led into a webinar on fixing the mistakes *(book)*.
- **Free list-building before ads:** guest on podcasts ("the best way is to get a free copy of my book… go to…") and guest-blog with a bio link *(book)*.
- **Publish daily** in one medium you can stand:
  - write a blog (Medium);
  - talk on a podcast (Brunson's "Marketing in Your Car", recorded on an iPhone while driving);
  - make a vlog.

  Or record once and repurpose into all three. **Document the journey**: you only need to be one or two steps ahead of your audience *(book)*.

**Workflow to build a lead funnel for a user:**
1. Define the audience's biggest pain, and pick one fast lead-magnet format from the nine.
2. Write 3–5 curiosity hooks, including at least one "# Biggest Mistakes" hook.
3. Draft the landing page: headline, magnet image, opt-in form. Keep fields minimal.
4. Draft the thank-you page: delivery, then a single next step (an SLO, a webinar, a podcast).
5. Draft a follow-up sequence (for example, day 0 delivery, day 2 story, day 4 offer), plus daily publishing.
6. Do the math: cost per lead, SLO conversion, break-even day, value per name per month.
7. Point to 2–3 similar funnels in the examples table to model.

## Caveats

- Follow email and SMS law (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, TCPA): get consent, include unsubscribe options, and disclose any affiliate links *(std)*.
- The book promotes ClickFunnels. The strategy works with any funnel or email tool.
- Revenue figures are illustrative, not guarantees *(book)*.
