# The Art of Focus (Dan Koe, 2023)

A working guide distilled from Dan Koe's *The Art of Focus* (KOE Press, 2023; limited keepsake edition) *(book)*. Its thesis: **focus is the catalyst to the good life**, and it is "dimension-agnostic", fuelling spiritual practice, business, relationships, health and writing *(book)*.

**Source:** the user's PDF has a text layer (231 pages). Points marked *(book)* were checked in it.

**Structure** *(book)*: Foreword (by a friend and business partner) · Introduction (a world of distractions) · **I Find Meaning**: The Principles, The 3 Pillars, The Universe · **II Reinvent Yourself**: The Self, The Game, The Lens, The Formula · **III Create Your Ideal Future**: The Shift, The New Rich, The Skill Stack, The Project, The Vessel · Resources and inspiration (Csikszentmihalyi, David Deida, Eckhart Tolle, Ken Wilber, Naval Ravikant, Alan Watts) · Turn Yourself Into A Business.

## Core ideas *(book)*

**The principles (Part I)** include: systems thinking; psychic entropy; content in consciousness; zooming out; self-experimentation; intelligent imitation; radical open-mindedness; observe and question; judgment vs discernment; radical acceptance; knowledge vs understanding; reality exploration; holistic synthesis; creativity vs productivity; storytelling; failure and struggle; iteration and persistence; ego development; **level of mind**; mastery over misery; tactical stress; selfishness as a priority.
- **Level of mind:** every problem demands an increase in your level of mind, meaning the openness of your perspective. Shed attachments to expectations and beliefs, then solve it through self-education, self-experimentation and persistence.

**The 3 pillars** (a mental feedback loop): **focus** (the first pillar; the common pattern of the successful; it has a frame, your perspective); **energy** ("energy flows where attention goes"; mental energy is attention, intention and will, and focus is its vessel); **experience** (what's right in front of you; maximize enjoyment by living in direct experience rather than overthinking past or future).

**The Game (Part II):** most people are "non-player characters" following an accepted web of expectations; by doing nothing you become a mental slave (attention, not labor). Games give clarity through a big goal and a descending series of smaller goals; "a plan creates clarity, and clarity creates action". Nobody can plan your life for you; learn the game, deconstruct it, and play with your own creativity.

**The Formula:**
1. **Anti-vision:** write in detail the life you refuse to live (how you don't want to look, feel and act; money that is too little; environments you'd hate) until you feel uncomfortable, then transmute that energy.
2. **Vision:** from the anti-vision, write the future you want; big, not limited by belief, emotionally compelling. "Dance between the two" to make better daily decisions.
3. **Purpose and the path of mastery:** turn the purpose into a big goal, then **yearly, monthly, weekly and daily goals** that create clarity, frame attention and repel distractions.
- Self-discipline is when the desire to become your highest version outweighs the desire for cheap pleasures.

**Part III, Create Your Ideal Future:**
- **Specific knowledge:** reclaim the childlike curiosity school strips away (credits Naval Ravikant).
- **Career creation** and **understanding the internet:** the internet gives individuals unlimited reach.
- **The new rich:** happiness is a skill to be practised, not given.
- **Lifestyle design** and **fill / empty / use:** design a daily system around how you fill your mind, empty it, and use it; do narrow-focused output in your highest-energy hours; treat it as a lifelong experiment.
- **The skill stack:** "specialized generalism". Become obsessed with one crevice of reality and stack skills to solve burning problems in it.
  - **The message:** relatable, understandable or actionable; you must learn to capture attention.
  - **The medium:** "in business, distribution equals freedom". Publish to the world.
  - **Results-oriented skills:** write, speak, market, sell. Message and medium are evergreen; results-oriented skills change with the landscape.
  - **Creative problem-solving.**
- **The project:** learn by doing (how to learn, become a builder); **project to product**: "Everyone is an entrepreneur; some just choose to get paid for the problems they solve in their personal life." You become an entrepreneur when you take your projects public. "You are the ultimate project."
- **The vessel:**
  - **The one-person business:** don't start a billion-dollar company; start as one person. Koe reports about $10K, $100K, $150K, $800K, then $3.5M in revenue over five years (rounded).
  - **You are the niche:** "the most profitable niche is you", your overlapping skills, interests and experience. Rather than "niche down" into work you hate, solve problems you've solved for yourself.
  - **Value creation:** information leads to awareness of opportunity, then valuable ideas, then behavior change that ripples outward.

## How to use this skill
- **Reinvention or direction:** run the formula with the user: anti-vision questions, then vision, then purpose, then yearly to daily goals.
- **Focus and productivity:** apply fill / empty / use and schedule deep work in peak-energy hours.
- **Turning interests into income:** skill stack (message, medium, results skills) → personal project → public product → one-person business built on "you are the niche".
- Keep quotes short and attribute ideas to Koe; much is philosophical, so present it as his perspective.
