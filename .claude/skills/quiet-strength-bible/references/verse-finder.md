# The Quiet Strength Verse Finder (pp. A11–A31)

The Verse Finder groups verses under the three P's of "a Winning Life": **I. Principles**, **II. Practices**, **III. Priorities** *(book)*.

Its introduction asks how to get a handle on God's principles, practices and priorities, since most of us "don't walk around with a divine voice in our head". God left us his Word in the Scriptures, a realistic picture of our human brokenness alongside his plan for help and healing *(book)*.

Each topic has:
- a key verse;
- a bold "principle statement";
- supporting references with one-line summaries.

References below were read from the page images. Some page-image text was faint, so treat a few references as approximate, and look up the passage before quoting it.

## III. PRIORITIES of a Winning Life (A30–A31)

**Knowing God through…** *(book)*:
- **God the Father:** we are all made with an emptiness only God can fill, but he wants us to come to him. James 4:8; Psalm 42:1–2; Matthew 6:9; 1 John 4:16.
- **God the Son, Jesus Christ:** Jesus calls us to come to him for forgiveness, friendship and fulfillment. John 1:14; John 7:37–38; John 14:6; Acts 3:15; Revelation 22:21.
- **God the Holy Spirit:** the Holy Spirit helps us understand God's love, convicts us of sin, protects our minds and teaches us spiritual truth. John 14:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10; John 16:13; Acts 1:8; Romans 8:11; Romans 15:16; Titus 3:5.

**Relationships and the Kingdom** *(book)*:
- **Friendship:** people are attracted to a person who is loyal, and that person makes more friends by being a good friend. Friendships should not compromise our faith. John 15:12–13; Proverbs 17:17; 2 Corinthians 6:14–18.
- **God's Kingdom:** humility, repentance and childlike faith are essential for entering it. We must have a second birth. Matthew 6:33; Matthew 18:2–3; John 3:3.
- **Marriage:** build your marriage on God's Word, and it will remain strong. Your marriage is one of your most important priorities. 1 Corinthians 7:14; Hebrews 13:4.
